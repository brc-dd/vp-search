## Walrus Notes

Walruses haul out on the pack ice in large mixed herds, and the herd tolerates a
remarkable amount of jostling before anyone objects. Observers keep meticulous
logs of the arrivals, the departures, the squabbles over the best slab, and the
long stretches where nothing happens at all. Somewhere in the middle of that
tedium sits buriedmarmot, far enough from either end of the paragraph that the
excerpt window has to elide both sides to reach it. The log continues for a
while afterwards, noting the tide, the wind, the drifting floes, and the slow
return of the herd to the water once the light finally goes.
